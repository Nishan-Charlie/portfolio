This is a guide to funding routes, not a live list of open calls. Dates, university participation and eligibility can change. An admission offer and a scholarship offer are separate unless the programme explicitly combines them.

Start here if you are applying from Sri Lanka

Country-specific routes: explore MEXT, GKS, Chinese Government Type A, Australia Awards, Fulbright, Chevening and Commonwealth. These use a Sri Lankan call or country-eligible process; they are not all exclusive to Sri Lankans.

International awards: Sri Lankan students can also compete for nationality-open university and international funding, subject to each programme's conditions. A “Sri Lanka route” label identifies the application channel, not a guarantee of selection.

Monitor Sri Lankan Ministry of Higher Education notices ↗ and the relevant embassy or award provider. A ministry nomination deadline can be earlier than the overseas deadline.

Universities & entry requirements
Examples to explore in Australia; programme rules control admission.
University / routeWhat to check

Monash University ↗PhD standard entry includes a relevant four-year research degree at honours IIA equivalent, or an eligible research master's. Research master's standard entry includes honours IIB equivalent, 65+; other assessed pathways exist.

How to prepare: For research degrees, document your thesis content and research contribution. For Australia Awards, connect your study plan to the current Sri Lanka priority fields and return-home development goals.

What you need

A working checklist, not a substitute for the award's official document list. Do not order medicals, police reports or paid credential evaluations until you know they are needed and will be valid at submission.

Tick items as you prepare. Keep your own copy of important records.

Application preparation checklist

A note on Sri Lankan qualifications and English waivers

Send the degree title, duration, transcript and grading scale to admissions if equivalence is unclear. A three-year general degree and a four-year honours degree may be assessed differently. Do not convert your own marks into a foreign GPA unless the application explicitly asks you to.

A medium-of-instruction letter is useful only when the university accepts it for a waiver. An English-taught degree does not automatically waive IELTS or TOEFL for every university or scholarship.

How to prepare

Suggested planning timeline, counted backwards from your earliest deadline. Start sooner if you need language testing, research experience or a nomination.

  1. 9–12 months before · decide your direction

    Choose taught master's, research master's or PhD. Shortlist courses or labs by fit, check degree equivalence, and build a table of admission and funding requirements.

  2. 6–9 months before · strengthen your evidence

    Complete a substantial project, thesis or relevant work. Prepare an academic CV, request transcripts, plan required tests and identify referees who know your work.

  3. 3–6 months before · tailor your application

    Read the programme guidance and recent lab work. Write specific statements and research plans. Contact supervisors where requested; follow office-led procedures where they are required.

  4. 1–3 months before · assemble and review

    Match every document to the checklist, confirm translations and references, check nomination steps, and rehearse explaining your project, contribution and future plans.

  5. Before the deadline · submit and confirm

    Submit early, check the time zone and save receipts. Make sure referees and any hard-copy documents arrive by their own deadlines. Monitor official messages for interviews or missing items.

For a master's application

Show that your previous study prepares you for the course. Explain the skills you want, why this curriculum fits, and how you will use it. For development awards, connect this to a specific need in Sri Lanka.

For a PhD application

Show research readiness: a clear question, relevant methods, evidence of your contribution and a plausible next step. Explain the fit with the supervisor or project. Publications can help, but the individual programme decides what evidence it requires.

How to approach a potential supervisor

First check whether direct contact is encouraged. Where appropriate, send a short, individual email: introduce your degree and research, explain a concrete connection to their recent work, suggest your question, and ask whether they expect to take funded students. Attach a concise CV and a relevant research summary if requested.

Keep the message specific and accurate. Supervisor interest is useful, but does not replace formal admission or a written funding offer.

Common questions

What GPA or IELTS score do I need?

There is no single country-wide score. The university, department and scholarship may set different requirements, including component scores. Use the linked entry rules and check how your Sri Lankan qualification is assessed. Meeting a minimum does not guarantee selection.

Do I need a master's before a PhD?

It depends on the programme. Some accept a relevant bachelor's with strong research preparation; others require a master's. Check the exact degree route, including integrated master's–PhD options.

Is every scholarship here fully funded?

No. Funding labels separate fee-and-living support, tuition-only awards, partial contributions and research employment. Read the offer for duration, renewal, insurance, international tuition, travel, dependants and any work or return-home conditions.

Can I apply to more than one scholarship?

Follow each scheme's rules. Some restrict track choices, course choices or holding other funding. You can prepare several suitable applications, but check restrictions before submitting or accepting awards.

Are these deadlines updated automatically?

No. This page was reviewed on 23 October 2026. Dates identify the cited cycle where available; they are not automatically rolled forward. Always open the official source for the current call, eligibility and exact closing time.
